Item formats you will encounter

Standard and Next Generation NCLEX formats

All item types may include charts, tables, graphics, or multimedia. Every exam also contains 15 unscored pretest items that look identical to scored items.

Multiple choice — single answer Standard
One clinical scenario, four answer options, one correct response. The most familiar format and still the most common on the exam.
Multiple response — select all that apply Standard
One or more correct answers from a list. Treat each option independently as true or false. Traditional SATA does not award partial credit; NGN versions may.
Ordered response / drag-and-drop Standard
Place all answer options in the correct sequence. There is one correct order.
Fill-in-the-blank Standard
Calculate and enter a numerical response, commonly for medication dosage, IV rates, and intake/output. An on-screen calculator is provided.
Hot spot, exhibit/chart, graphic options, and audio Standard
These formats require selecting a location on an image, interpreting tabbed patient data, choosing from image-based options, or identifying a clinical sound using provided headphones.
Clinical judgment case studies NGN
Six-item sets built around an evolving patient scenario. Each item targets one step of the NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model: recognize cues, analyze cues, prioritize hypotheses, generate solutions, take action, and evaluate outcomes.
Matrix/grid and highlight NGN
Matrix items ask you to make linked decisions across rows and columns. Highlight items ask you to mark the clinically relevant words or phrases in a passage.
Trend and bowtie NGN
Trend items require interpretation of data across time. Bowtie items connect patient conditions, nursing actions, and expected outcomes around a central clinical problem.

What changed — and what did not — in 2026

The structure stayed the same; performance expectations were refined.

Structure and scoring: no changes

The CAT format, NGN item types, content categories, percentage weights, and pass/fail scoring method are unchanged from the previous cycle.

Unchanged
Competency statements refined

Activity statements were updated based on the 2024 RN Practice Analysis. Clinical judgment, ethical care, and equity are more explicitly named as performance expectations across categories.

Strengthened
Health equity and culturally responsive care strengthened

Explicit expectations around unbiased treatment, equal access, and culturally responsive practice now appear more clearly across activity statements.

Strengthened
Technology as a reasoning context

Technology is not a standalone category. It appears within scenarios involving EHR interpretation, digital documentation, telehealth decisions, and confidentiality on social media.

New context
Professional accountability strengthened

Ethical decision-making, patient rights, safety, and risk prevention are stated more explicitly. The nurse is expected to act as a final safeguard for patient safety.
