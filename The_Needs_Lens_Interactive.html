Maslow's hierarchy
Tier 1 of 5
Physiological Needs
What this tier includes
Oxygen, nutrition, fluid balance, elimination, thermoregulation, rest, and physical comfort. These are the body's most immediate survival requirements.
NCLEX application rule
When a physiological need is unmet or threatened, it takes priority over all other needs. An unmet physiological need that represents an immediate threat to life is governed by ABCs. An unmet physiological need that does not represent an active life threat is still addressed before safety or psychosocial needs.
Annotated example
A nurse is caring for two clients. The first has post-operative pain rated seven out of ten and is requesting medication. The second has not voided in ten hours and reports lower abdominal discomfort.

Which client takes priority?
The second client. Urinary retention after ten hours represents an unmet physiological need with a defined clinical threshold. Post-operative pain is also physiological, but urinary retention carries greater immediate physiological risk.

A Note on How This Framework Travels
Maslow's hierarchy is familiar across Latin American nursing programs. On the NCLEX, the challenge is speed and precision: identify which tier is unmet right now, then choose the option that addresses it directly.
